Where A Pint of Stout Meets A Box of Screws.

Welcome to Foxy John’s — Dingle’s legendary half-pub, half-hardware store and heritage bicycle shop. For over 120 years, locals and travellers have gathered on Main Street for creamy pints of Guinness, authentic Kerry craic, and a few spanners to take home.

Notice on Dining & Online Ordering

Foxy John's is an authentic, traditional Irish wet pub, hardware merchant, and bicycle hire shop. In keeping with our 120-year community heritage, we do NOT operate a restaurant kitchen, table booking platform, takeaway service, or online food ordering. All drinks, merchandise, and bicycles are handled on a welcoming, first-come, first-served walk-in basis right at the counter. For general questions or group bike hire enquiries, please phone us directly at +353 66 915 1316.

Plan A Walk-In Visit
A Dingle Institution

Half Traditional Pub. Half Hardware Store.

Step through the red front door on Main Street and you'll find beer taps pouring creamy stout to your left, and wooden shelves stacked with hammers, nails, spanners, and rat traps to your right. Explore what makes Foxy John's one of the world's most unique drinking spots.

Working Hardware

The Hardware Counter

Need a box of two-inch screws, an adjustable spanner, rubber wellies, or a roll of twine while nursing a pint? Our hardware side has supplied Dingle craftsmen and farmers since the 19th century. Everything has a price tag, right from the ceiling down to the wooden bins.

🔨 Tools, fasteners, and gear sold over the bar
The Black Stuff

The Front Bar & Taps

A narrow central alley of rich timber and low stools. The taps pour cold, velvety Guinness and Murphy's with meticulous two-part pours, alongside local craft beer, crisp ciders, and top-shelf Irish whiskeys like Redbreast and Dingle Single Malt.

🍺 Voted among Dingle's finest pints of stout
Historic Snug

The Legendary Snug

Tucked immediately left of the front door is the snug — a private, timber-panelled booth with a front window onto Main Street and its own private drink hatch opening directly to the bar. Snagging this coveted spot early is a true Dingle triumph.

🚪 Private drink hatch • First come, first served
Peat Fireplace

The Roaring Hearth Room

Step through to the back room dominated by an open stone fireplace burning fragrant Kerry turf (peat). The warm crackle of burning sods, stone walls, and cozy corners provide the perfect sanctuary on a crisp Atlantic evening.

🔥 Real turf fire burning daily in cooler weather
Beer Garden

The Courtyard & Beer Garden

Past the hearth lies our open-air courtyard patio. On sunny Kerry afternoons, it's filled with good banter, fresh air, and often our friendly resident sheepdog, who is always ready for a game of fetch with patrons enjoying a cold drink.

🐕 Dog-friendly inside and out • Open-air patio
Peninsula Touring

Heritage Bicycle Hire

For decades, visitors have rented reliable touring bicycles from Foxy John's to cycle the world-renowned Slea Head Drive. Grab your bike, a helmet, a route map, and set off along the breathtaking Wild Atlantic Way coastline.

🚲 Rental bikes, helmets & repair kits on site

Wild Atlantic Craic

Traditional Irish Music Amongst the Tools

Music is woven into the soul of Dingle. At Foxy John’s, musicians often squeeze right behind the hardware counter or gather around the peat hearth to play reels, jigs, and ballads. You’ll hear fiddles, accordions, tin whistles, guitars, and the steady heartbeat of the bodhrán.

When To Catch A Session

Impromptu and scheduled trad sessions occur regularly in the evenings, especially from Thursday through Sunday and throughout festival weekends. As sessions can kick off spontaneously, call in early, grab a stool, and let the melodies carry the night.

Arrive Early
Seats near the musicians fill fast. Arrive before 8:30 PM to secure a stool.
Session Etiquette
Listen closely during quiet tunes, join the chorus on lively ballads, and buy a round.

The Slea Head Coastal Route

A breathtaking 30-mile journey starting in Dingle, passing Ventry beach, ancient beehive huts, Dunmore Head (the westernmost point of mainland Europe), and offering panoramic vistas of the Blasket Islands before returning to Foxy John’s for a triumphant pint.

• Distance: ~47 km (30 miles) • Suggested Time: 3.5 – 5 hours
